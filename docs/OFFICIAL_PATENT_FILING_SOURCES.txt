OFFICIAL INDIA PATENT FILING DOCUMENTS
======================================

These source files were downloaded from the official Intellectual Property
India website and added to data/corpus/ip_india/guidelines/:

1. patent_office_filing_manual.pdf
   Official URL:
   https://ipindia.gov.in/frontend/pdf/patents/Manual_for_Patent_Office_Practice_and_Procedure_.pdf
   Purpose: Patent Office practice and procedure, including filing guidance and
   documents commonly required for a patent application.

2. patent_filing_forms_schedule_1.pdf
   Official URL:
   https://ipindia.gov.in/frontend/pdf/forms_and_official_fees/Schedule_1.pdf
   Purpose: Official schedule describing prescribed patent forms and fees under
   the Patents Rules, 2003.

Official index page:
https://www.ipindia.gov.in/other-publications

These are guidance and prescribed-form sources, not a patent grant certificate
and not a certificate for a particular invention. The exact documents required
depend on the application type, applicant status, inventorship, priority,
biological-resource disclosure, drawings, sequence listings, and other facts.
The assistant must present them as a checklist for verification, not as a
guarantee that filing requirements are complete for every case.
