# Corpus Quarantine

`corpus-invalid-2026-09-06/` contains source files excluded from authoritative
retrieval because they were empty, had invalid PDF signatures, or duplicated
another source's content.

The files are preserved for replacement and provenance review. They must not be
copied back into `data/corpus` until validated against the authoritative source
and assigned a source URL, checksum, document type, jurisdiction, and authority
tier.
